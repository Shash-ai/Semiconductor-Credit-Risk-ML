# Bank Demo Script

## Recommended Demo Duration

10-12 minutes.

---

## 0:00-1:00 — Problem

"India is expanding semiconductor manufacturing capacity, but banks face a difficult risk-assessment problem because there is limited domestic historical default data for these projects."

Explain that the project therefore focuses on decision support rather than unsupported default prediction.

---

## 1:00-2:00 — Dataset

Show:

- 12 manufacturing projects,
- 24 semiconductor design projects,
- verified public sources,
- RBI sector-level banking indicators.

Explain the difference between manufacturing investment and design-project outlay.

---

## 2:00-3:30 — Machine Learning

Show:

- PCA,
- validated clustering,
- structural segmentation.

Explain:

"These clusters identify structurally similar projects. They are not default or risk classes."

---

## 3:30-5:00 — Stress Testing

Show:

- baseline risk,
- mild scenario,
- moderate scenario,
- severe scenario.

Then show Monte Carlo tail-risk results.

Explain:

"This helps identify projects that are more sensitive to adverse sector and banking conditions."

---

## 5:00-6:30 — Borrower Evidence

Show:

- available borrower financial evidence,
- credit-rating evidence,
- information gaps.

Explain that unavailable financial information is not treated as poor credit quality.

---

## 6:30-8:00 — Bank Decision Support

Show:

- project stress vulnerability,
- concentration impact,
- indicative A-E research grade,
- credit posture,
- exposure posture,
- monitoring priority.

Emphasize:

"The A-E category is a research decision-support grade, not an official credit rating."

---

## 8:00-9:00 — Early Warning

Show:

- GREEN,
- AMBER,
- RED.

Explain how this can support monitoring and escalation.

---

## 9:00-10:00 — Portfolio Allocation

Show the constrained allocation analysis.

Explain:

"We tested exposure policies across 162 scenarios to evaluate ranking and allocation robustness."

Do not describe modelled stress reduction as actual default reduction.

---

## 10:00-11:00 — Limitations

Clearly state:

- no loan-level default history,
- no PD/LGD/EAD,
- research prototype,
- real bank validation still required.

---

## 11:00-12:00 — Pilot Ask

Conclude with:

"We would like to validate this framework with a small number of anonymized historical cases and obtain structured feedback from your credit-risk professionals."
