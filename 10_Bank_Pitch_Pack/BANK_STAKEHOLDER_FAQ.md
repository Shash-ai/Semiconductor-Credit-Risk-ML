# Bank Stakeholder FAQ

## 1. Does the system predict default?

No.

The current framework does not estimate Probability of Default because a defensible historical semiconductor project-level default dataset is not currently available.

---

## 2. Why call it machine learning?

Machine learning is used for structural segmentation and pattern discovery, while additional analytical layers perform stress testing, Monte Carlo simulation, financial analysis and portfolio optimization.

---

## 3. Why not train XGBoost or Random Forest?

Using supervised classification without reliable observed target labels would create misleading model performance.

The framework intentionally avoids fabricated default labels.

---

## 4. What is the A-E grade?

It is an indicative research decision-support category designed to summarize relative analytical risk evidence.

It is not an official bank internal rating or external credit rating.

---

## 5. Can this approve or reject a loan?

No.

The system supports human credit analysis only.

---

## 6. Can banks use their own internal data?

Potentially yes, in a controlled pilot.

Relevant data could include:

- internal credit grades,
- exposure,
- repayment behaviour,
- collateral,
- covenants,
- project implementation status,
- borrower financials.

---

## 7. What happens if real default data becomes available?

The framework could be extended into supervised PD modelling and backtesting.

---

## 8. Is the model production ready?

No.

It is currently a research prototype / pre-pilot decision-support framework.

---

## 9. What is the main research contribution?

The framework combines:

- semiconductor project-level evidence,
- macro-financial banking indicators,
- unsupervised ML,
- stress testing,
- Monte Carlo simulation,
- borrower evidence,
- portfolio optimization,
- bank credit decision support.

---

## 10. Why semiconductor credit risk?

The sector involves very large capital commitments, policy dependence, technology uncertainty and limited historical Indian project-credit performance data, making it suitable for a specialized analytical framework.
