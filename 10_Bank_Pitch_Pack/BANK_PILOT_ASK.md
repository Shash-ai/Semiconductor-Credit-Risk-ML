# Proposed Bank Pilot

## What We Are Asking From the Bank

We are not requesting immediate production deployment.

We propose a small controlled validation exercise.

---

## Suggested Pilot

### Participants

2-5 banking professionals from areas such as:

- credit risk,
- corporate banking,
- project finance,
- portfolio risk,
- credit analytics.

### Data

Preferably:

- anonymized,
- historical,
- non-production,
- semiconductor/electronics-related credit cases.

### Initial Sample

Approximately 5-15 exposures would be sufficient for an exploratory pilot.

---

## What We Would Compare

For each case:

1. Existing analyst assessment
2. Existing internal grade, where permitted
3. Framework-generated risk signals
4. Stress-test output
5. Concentration output
6. Reviewer judgement

---

## What We Want to Learn

- Are the model outputs relevant?
- Are the results understandable?
- Do analysts agree directionally with the signals?
- What risk factors are missing?
- What bank data should be incorporated?
- Which outputs are useful or unnecessary?
- Could the framework improve preliminary credit review?

---

## What the Bank Receives

A pilot report containing:

- project-level analytical results,
- risk drivers,
- stress-testing output,
- model limitations,
- comparison with analyst feedback,
- recommendations for further development.

---

## Data Protection Principle

No customer-identifying information is required for the exploratory pilot.

The research team should only use minimum necessary and appropriately anonymized data.
