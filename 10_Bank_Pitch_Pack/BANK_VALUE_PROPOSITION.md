# Bank Value Proposition

## Core Value Proposition

The framework aims to help banks evaluate semiconductor-sector exposures more consistently by combining borrower, project, sector and portfolio-level risk evidence in one decision-support environment.

---

## Potential Benefits

### 1. Better Preliminary Credit Screening

Provides a structured first-level view of project vulnerability before detailed underwriting.

### 2. Project-Level Stress Testing

Shows how semiconductor projects may behave under adverse macro-financial conditions.

### 3. Concentration Awareness

Highlights large project exposure and geographic concentration.

### 4. Portfolio Allocation Support

Allows banks to test different exposure limits and allocation policies.

### 5. Early-Warning Monitoring

Generates monitoring priorities and GREEN / AMBER / RED research indicators.

### 6. Explainability

Outputs are designed to show:

- major risk drivers,
- major mitigants,
- borrower evidence,
- stress sensitivity,
- concentration impact.

### 7. Human-in-the-Loop Design

The tool supports analysts rather than replacing them.

---

## Potential Banking Use Cases

- semiconductor project finance,
- corporate lending,
- sector-specific exposure review,
- portfolio concentration analysis,
- credit-committee preparation,
- stress-testing research,
- policy-linked lending assessment.

---

## Why Banks May Care

India's semiconductor ecosystem is developing quickly, while historical domestic project-default data remains limited.

This creates a gap between:

- industrial-policy growth,
- high capital requirements,
- bank-credit risk assessment.

The framework is designed to address that gap as a sector-specific analytical layer.
