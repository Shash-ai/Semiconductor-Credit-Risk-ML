# Semiconductor Credit Risk Decision-Support Platform

## What problem does it solve?

Banks financing semiconductor projects face a difficult credit-assessment environment:

- limited historical sector-specific default data,
- very high project capital requirements,
- long gestation periods,
- technology and execution uncertainty,
- geographic and borrower concentration,
- rapidly evolving policy and industry conditions.

Traditional borrower-level financial analysis alone may not capture these project-level and sector-level risks.

---

## What is the proposed solution?

A machine-learning-enabled bank credit decision-support framework combining:

- verified semiconductor project data,
- borrower financial evidence,
- macro-financial banking indicators,
- machine-learning-based structural segmentation,
- deterministic stress testing,
- Monte Carlo tail-risk analysis,
- portfolio concentration analysis,
- constrained credit-allocation optimization,
- indicative internal risk grades,
- early-warning signals.

---

## Who is it for?

Potential users include:

- corporate banking teams,
- project finance teams,
- credit-risk analysts,
- credit committees,
- portfolio-risk teams,
- sector-risk research teams.

---

## What does it produce?

For each semiconductor exposure, the framework can provide:

- relative project vulnerability,
- stress sensitivity,
- tail-risk indicators,
- borrower-strength evidence,
- concentration impact,
- indicative research risk grade,
- credit review posture,
- exposure posture,
- monitoring priority,
- GREEN / AMBER / RED early-warning status.

---

## Why is it different?

The framework does not fabricate default labels where sector-specific historical default data is unavailable.

Instead, it combines transparent machine learning, financial analysis, stress testing and portfolio-risk logic to support human credit judgement.

---

## Current Status

Research prototype / pre-pilot stage.

The framework is intended for controlled validation with banking professionals before any production use.

---

## Important Boundary

This system is not:

- an automated loan approval engine,
- a regulatory PD model,
- a credit-rating-agency rating,
- an Expected Credit Loss model.

Human credit judgement remains mandatory.
